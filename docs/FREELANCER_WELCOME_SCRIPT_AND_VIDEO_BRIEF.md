# POBlancer freelancer welcome video

Prepared 28 October 2026. Intended for the freelancer registration page only.

## Ready-to-record narration

Aim for about two minutes at a clear, conversational pace. Allow up to two minutes twenty seconds rather than rushing the explanation.

Hello, welcome to POBlancer. I’m glad you’re here. Before you sign up, let me explain how we work.

Clients come to us for digital and creative services, from websites and applications to videos, design and marketing. Our promise is quality work, clear communication and delivery as agreed.

Choose the category that honestly describes you.

Rising Talent Freelancers are starting their professional journey. Be open about your skills and experience, and begin with affordable prices that match what you can deliver.

Independent Freelancers are professionals working under their own name and reputation. Experienced experts are welcome, and you can apply to join the POB team later.

POB Internally Trained Freelancers have trained with us. POB Experienced Freelancers bring existing professional experience and are approved to work under our brand. Both follow our quality and conduct standards, and your profile shows your category.

As a POB freelancer, you join a private community where we support each other. We plan to offer featured promotion, and POB can assign client work directly when suitable work is available. Paid advertising plans are also available to freelancers who want more visibility.

You agree your deadline with the client. Choose a realistic date. Our target is to finish the main work by 70% of that time. For a ten-day project, that means day seven, leaving three days for review, corrections and final delivery.

Ask a community member to review your work. If you need help, speak up early, use Project Support in your dashboard, and keep the client informed. Agree the remaining work and the supporting freelancer’s share before they start. You can also support someone else and earn an agreed share.

Before choosing a POB category, open our Terms and Conditions, download the agreement for your category, read it, sign it and follow the submission instructions.

Choose honestly, communicate early, and deliver what you promise. Welcome to POBlancer.

## Exact explanations the video must preserve

- The milestone is 70% of elapsed project time, not 70% before the deadline and not a 70% completion score. Ten days means main-work target on day seven, with three days remaining.
- The freelancer agrees a realistic delivery date with the client. The review target does not change that client deadline.
- Ask for help as soon as a delay becomes likely. Do not wait until the final day.
- The remaining time supports review and corrections. Do not imply that every project lasts ten days or always has three review days.
- Community support applies to approved POB Internally Trained and POB Experienced Freelancers. Do not depict all independent accounts as automatically enrolled in the private community.
- Joining POB involves approval and the relevant signed agreement. A dropdown selection alone is not proof of approval, training or verification.
- Independent experts may apply to join the POB team later. Rising Talent must describe their ability honestly and price in line with that ability.
- Supporters agree the scope, deadline and earnings share before work starts. The current system stores agreements and allocation records; do not claim that the platform automatically transfers or splits payments.
- Keep the client informed about progress, challenges and any expected delay. Do not share client credentials or private files in a public support offer.
- Promotion and direct assignments depend on availability and eligibility. Do not add a promise of guaranteed income, a fixed number of jobs, unlimited free advertising or a guaranteed response time.
- Describe future featured promotion as planned. Do not depict it as an already active universal entitlement.
